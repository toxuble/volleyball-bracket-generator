Automatic allocation

Available play time is split among rounds according to court waves and increasing match lengths. Later rounds receive longer match slots by default, with the championship longest. Each round gets at least five minutes per court wave. Rounding is balanced so the last round ends exactly where the buffer begins.

Choosing points and sets

The planner searches one-set and best-of-3, 5, 7, or longer odd-numbered formats, with targets from 5 to 21 points per set. It fits estimated playing time to the allotted match slot, with a small preference for fewer sets and familiar point targets. Point targets never exceed 21. Longer slots use more sets; win-by-two may extend the actual score beyond the target. Best-of formats end as soon as a team wins a majority of sets. Each set is win by two without a hard scoring cap.

Verified against USA Volleyball 2025–2027 tournament scheduling guidance, page 132. A set to 15 has a 20-minute allowance including six minutes of warm-up, giving a 14-minute planning benchmark. Two sets to 21 have a 45-minute allowance; subtracting six minutes of warm-up and a three-minute interval gives about 18 minutes per set. Other point targets are interpolated or extrapolated, not measured durations. Multi-set estimates assume equally matched teams: best-of-3 averages 2.5 sets, and best-of-5 averages 4.125. These are scheduling estimates, not guaranteed game lengths. Short sets below 15 points have no direct timing benchmark in that source. Teams may play fewer or more sets. Inter-set breaks are excluded.

Adjusting the timeline

Drag an orange boundary bar to transfer minutes between adjacent rounds, or focus it and use arrow keys (Shift changes five minutes). Only the boundary bars adjust the timeline. Points and sets recalculate automatically. The total window and buffer stay fixed. Reset timeline restores the automatic allocation and keeps team names, positions, and winners.

Courts and actual finish times

Matches run in simultaneous court waves; each round finishes before the next begins. Byes take no time. Team size changes the roster total, not the timing estimate. Scheduled slots fill the window exactly, but point-based play can finish earlier or later; the buffer allows for overruns. Custom points and set formats are organizer rules.
